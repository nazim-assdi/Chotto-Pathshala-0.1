# Gyanbari Curriculum Design Brief

## Purpose

Create a coherent, original early-years and primary-foundation learning journey for children in Bangladesh across Playgroup, Nursery, KG and Class One. The app should build understanding, language, number sense, curiosity, independence and safe everyday habits—not merely memorise isolated facts.

This document is a design brief, not a completed curriculum map or a claim of certification by any named organisation.

## Reference frameworks and how to use them

| Reference | Useful design principles | Gyanbari adaptation |
|---|---|---|
| Cambridge Early Years / Primary | Clear learning progression, subject knowledge, communication and reasoning | Age-banded outcomes and gradual progression from recognition to explanation |
| IB Primary Years Programme (PYP) | Inquiry, learner agency, concepts and connections across subjects | Short guided questions, observation tasks and cross-subject mini-projects |
| Montessori | Concrete materials, repetition, choice, sensory exploration and independence | Picture cards, sorting, matching, tracing and self-paced practice |
| UNICEF foundational learning | Foundational literacy, numeracy, inclusion and child-centred learning | Early reading, number sense, accessible instructions and frequent low-stakes practice |
| EYFS | Prime learning areas, play-based learning and developmentally appropriate milestones | Play, communication, physical development, personal/social skills and early knowledge |

Use publicly described principles and learning goals only. Do not copy proprietary lesson text, worksheets, images, question banks, or textbook passages. Do not imply endorsement or accreditation.

## Learning strands

1. **Bangla language and literacy:** listening, speaking, vocabulary, rhymes/oral language where rights permit, letter recognition, sound awareness, reading readiness, handwriting readiness and comprehension.
2. **English language:** listening, speaking, phonics readiness, alphabet, vocabulary, simple phrases, reading readiness and early writing.
3. **Mathematics:** sorting, matching, patterns, shapes, spatial language, counting, number recognition, comparison, addition/subtraction foundations, measurement, time and money concepts.
4. **Environmental studies and early science:** self, family, plants, animals, weather, water, materials, senses, health, local environment and simple observation.
5. **Arabic and Islamic/moral learning:** age-appropriate Arabic letter recognition and pronunciation, everyday manners, values and carefully reviewed learning content. Religious text and pronunciation must be checked by qualified reviewers.
6. **Creative expression:** drawing, colour, music/rhythm activities where appropriate, craft, imagination and storytelling.
7. **Health, safety and life skills:** hygiene, nutrition, body awareness, road and home safety, emotional vocabulary, cooperation and asking trusted adults for help.
8. **Digital and thinking skills:** following simple instructions, sequencing, classification, problem-solving and safe, supervised interaction.

## Progression by level

### Playgroup
Prioritise play, oral language, sensory exploration, matching, sorting, body movement, social interaction, colour/shape recognition and counting experiences. Keep activities short and instructions concrete.

### Nursery
Build vocabulary, sound awareness, pre-writing control, number recognition, counting, patterns, shapes, nature observation, self-care and simple explanations.

### KG
Strengthen letter-sound links, early reading, early writing, number sense, simple operations, classification, observation, sequencing and independent task completion.

### Class One
Provide a structured bridge to primary learning: age-appropriate reading comprehension, sentence formation, foundational mathematics, environmental/science concepts, problem-solving, creative work and responsible habits. Align local language and subject terminology with Bangladesh school context.

## Lesson template

Every lesson should contain:
- Stable lesson ID, level, subject, sequence number and title.
- One to three measurable learning outcomes.
- Prerequisite or revision prompt where useful.
- Short child-friendly explanation or story.
- Concrete examples with clear picture metadata.
- Guided activity and independent practice.
- Answer key and a brief explanation, not just the correct option.
- A quick check or quiz.
- Optional parent/teacher facilitation tip.
- Accessibility notes and age-appropriate vocabulary.
- Audio text segments with language tags and pronunciation review status.

## Assessment and quality standards

- Questions must have one defensible answer unless explicitly marked open-ended.
- Distractors should be plausible but not confusing or unfair.
- Avoid trick questions, stereotypes, unsafe imitation and shame-based feedback.
- Vary question types: recognition, matching, sequencing, classification, application and simple explanation.
- Track mastery by learning outcome, not only total score.
- Review Bangla spelling, English usage, Arabic script/pronunciation, factual accuracy, cultural suitability and image-to-word matching.
- Use original illustrations or assets with documented licences and alt text.
- Make text, audio and visuals reinforce the same concept.

## Audio and accessibility requirements

- Each content segment should carry an explicit language code (for example `bn-BD`, `en-US`, `ar-SA`) rather than guessing language from the entire lesson.
- Provide slow, normal and fast playback without changing the lesson text.
- Keep narration optional and provide visible text for every spoken instruction.
- Audio downloads require informed consent, clear file-size information, progress, retry and storage management.
- Offline playback must use verified local files. If no cached recording or compatible offline TTS voice exists, explain the limitation and offer an online fallback only when the user has enabled it.

## Implementation status

This brief establishes the target design. It does not mean all strands, lessons, reviewed audio, image assets or offline audio packs are already implemented. Content should be added and validated incrementally while preserving existing lesson and quiz records.
