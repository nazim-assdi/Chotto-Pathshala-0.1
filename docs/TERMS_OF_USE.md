The YouTube video feature is disabled in the default app build. It may be enabled only after the app owner completes the required child-directed API Client notification and privacy setup, and after each video passes the app's individual review for age suitability, current Made for Kids status, embeddability and rights.

Before a user accesses the YouTube player, the app asks the user to review and accept this app's Privacy Policy and YouTube's Terms of Service. **By using the YouTube video feature, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.**

- [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms)

The player is configured with autoplay disabled and keeps YouTube's own controls and branding visible. YouTube controls its own player, supplied advertising, related-video experience and processing under its terms and privacy policy.

Videos are streamed by YouTube. Chotto Pathshala does not download or cache YouTube audiovisual content and does not provide background or offline playback. Availability and playback restrictions are determined by YouTube and the rights holder.
